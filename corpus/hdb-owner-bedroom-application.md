# Application Process for Renting Out Bedrooms

Before applying for approval to rent out your bedrooms, familiarise yourself with these [terms and conditions](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/apply-manage-and-renew-rental-of-bedroom/terms-and-conditions). You must seek HDB’s approval through [MyHDB Page](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/myhdb-page) or [MyHDB mobile app](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/myhdb-mobile-app), before the commencement of the tenancy.

Donees named in the Lasting Power of Attorney or Court-appointed Deputy may [write to us](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/write-to-us) to apply for renting out of bedrooms.

You also need to provide your tenants’ particulars and other related details at the time of registration. An administrative fee of $9 for each bedroom is payable upon approval.

After your rooms have been rented, you must notify us within 7 days, if there is:

- Termination or renewal of the rental
- Change to your tenants or tenants’ particulars

For inclusion of new proposed tenants, flat owners must obtain HDB’s approval before their new tenants may move into the flat.

### **e-Services for renting out of bedrooms**

You can register the rental online and update registration details at [MyHDB Page](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/myhdb-page) (log in using your Singpass and go to: My Flat > Purchased Flat > Renting Out) via the following e-Services:

- [Commencement of renting out bedrooms](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR12AWPortal)
- [Termination of renting out bedrooms](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR12AWPortal)
- [Renewal of renting out bedrooms](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR12AWPortal)
- [Change of tenants/ tenants’ particulars (for renting out bedrooms)](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR12AWPortal)

You can also print a confirmation letter showing the names of tenants registered at the flat via the '[Enquiry on Tenants](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR12AWPortal)' e-Service. Alternatively, you may use the ‘[Enquiry on Authorised Tenants](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR12AWEnquireTenant/BR12PEnquirer.jsp)’ e-Service to assure your tenants that they have been registered with HDB.
