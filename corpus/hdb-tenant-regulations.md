# Regulations for Renting a Flat or a Bedroom from Open Market

## Non-citizen (NC) quota for renting out of flat

If you are a non-Malaysian NC (Singapore permanent resident or foreigner) looking to rent a flat, you will need to consider the NC quota. If the quota is reached, only Singaporeans and Malaysians can rent a flat in that neighbourhood or block. The quota does not apply if the family renting the flat includes at least 1 Singaporean and all unrelated tenants are Singaporeans or Malaysians.

The quota aims to prevent the formation of foreigner enclaves in public housing estates. Malaysians are not subject to this quota in view of their close cultural and historical similarities with Singaporeans. The quota is set at 8% at the neighbourhood level and 11% at the block level. This quota does not apply to the rental of bedrooms.

You may use our [e-Service](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR12AWNCQuota/) to check if an HDB flat can be rented by non-Malaysian NCs. The e-Service can also be accessed via the [HDB Map](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/hdb-map).

## Rental period

You must rent an HDB flat/ bedroom for at least 6 months. Owners can apply to rent out flat/ bedrooms for a maximum period of 3 years per application if their tenants are all Singaporeans or Malaysians. For application involving non-Malaysian NCs, the maximum rental period per approval is 2 years.

## Maximum number of tenants and occupants

Take note of the maximum number of tenants and occupants allowed in each flat when renting the flat/ bedrooms.

|  | **Renting of Flat** | **Renting of Bedroom(s)** |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flat Type** | **Maximum Number of Tenants Allowed in Each Flat** | **Maximum Number of Bedroom(s) Allowed** | **Maximum Number of Occupants* Allowed in Each Flat**** |
| 1-room and 2-room | 4 | Owners are not allowed to rent out the bedroom. |  |
| 3-room | 6 | 1 | 6 |
| 4-room and bigger^ | 8 | 2 | 8 |

* Occupants include owners, authorised occupiers and tenants. The maximum number of occupants applies even if flat owners rent out bedroom(s) to tenants who are from the same family unit.

** Only bedrooms originally constructed by HDB can be rented out. All other parts of the flat (including partitioned rooms) cannot be used as bedrooms for tenants.

^ The number of tenants/ occupants allowed for 4-room and bigger flats will revert to a maximum of 6 persons for rental period extending beyond 31 December 2028.
